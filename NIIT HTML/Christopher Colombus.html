Who was Christopher Colombus?

Christopher Colombus was an italian explorer. He was born in AD 1451 in Genoa. He was greatly impressed by Marco Polo's book on his travels in China. He had also heard about the riches of India and wanted to see it for himself.Columbus set sail from Spain with three ships the Pinta, the Nina and the Santa Marina, hoping to find a new sea route to India. In those days, most of the explorers sailed south of Europe, going around Africa. this was a long juorney and took years to complete. Colombus decided to sailwest across the Atlantis Ocean. He thought he would reach China in a few days.

But, the voyage took much longer than he thought. With foood and water running short, his sailors finally spotted land after 36 days.

However, "instead of reaching China as he had planned, Colombus had actually landed in the Bahamas- islands located near North America. Colombus thought that he had reached India and called the place the 'Indies' and called the people 'Indians'.

These islands are still called the West Indies, and the people West Indians. The original peopleof North America were, for a long time, called the Red Indians.

Colombus spent three months exploring the new land, but he never saw gold and wealth of Asia as described by Marco Polo.Till The end of his life Colombus never knew he had discovered a new continent.

It was only much later when Amerigo Vespucci explored the coast of South America that people realised that Colombus had found was indeed a 'New World'.

Colombus changed history in many ways. It is because of the voyages of Colombus that the Europeans could conquer large parts of North and South America. He sailed with little but with stars and a compass to guide. His courage inspired many explorer to follow him.
