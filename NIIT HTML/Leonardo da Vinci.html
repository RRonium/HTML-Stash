Leonardo da Vinci




Contribution

One of the most famous artists of the Renaissance was Leonardo da Vinci. He was an artist, scientist and inventor. He was called the Renaissance Man (one who could do many things very well.) Two of his best known painting are "The Last Supper" and Mona Lisa.

He was very curious about the world around him, and invented many new devices. He was as intrested in engineering as he was in art. He drew pictures and designs of helicopters, war machines, musical instruments, water pumps and more.
