Marco Polo

Contribution

Marco Polo was an explorer and a trader born in Venice. He was one of the first Europeans to travel
into Mongolia and China. He became known for his book "The Travels of Marco Polo", that told the story of his travels along the Silk road to China.

Unlike the later explorers, Marco Polo did not find a new sea-route to th East. But the stories were a
source of inspiration. for many of the later explorers.

Marco Polo was born in Venice in Italy in AD 1254. He spent 25 years travelling around the world with his father and uncle, who were traders. They are believed to have spent 17 years in China with the Chinese king Kublai Khan. They saw a wealth of gold, pearls, diamonds and rubies in China. They faced many adventures during their travels. They even had to escape from tigers and banditson their way! On reurning to Venice, Marco Polo became a storyteller and was called 'the man with many stories'. But many people laughed and called him a liar because his stories were so fabulous.
War broke out in Venice and Marco Polo was taken prisoner. A fellow prisoner wrote down the stories in a book and called it The Travels of Marco Polo.
On his deathbed, Marco Polo was asked by his priest to admit that the stories he had told people about his travels were lies. Marco refused and replied "I have not told you half of what I saw because you would not believe me."
