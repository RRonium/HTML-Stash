The Renaissance


The word 'renaissance' means 'rebirth'. This movement started in Italy in AD 1350 and soon spread across Europe. The Renaissance marks the end of theMiddle Ages and start of the Modern Age in Europe.
The Renaissance was one of the most creative periods in human history. Artists and scholars rediscovered the classical art and culture of ancient Rome and Greece. People once again started experimenting with science. They started thinking and questioning.
During the Renaissance, art and architecture flourished. There were new inventions and discoveries in the fields of science, astronomy and mathematics. Many thinkers were born.
People began to question age old beliefs and look for answers. Scientists and inventors began to study things around them. Looking up at the night sky they wondered if the Earth was really at the centre of the universe, as they had been taught by the church.
